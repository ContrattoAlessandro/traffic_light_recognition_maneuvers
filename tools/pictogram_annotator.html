Item Annotated color:

Shape of the lit lamp with the annotated color

Guidelines
  • Label the lamp inside the magenta box that is lit with the annotated color. If the box holds a whole head with several lit lamps, label the one with that color.
  • circle: a full round lamp, no symbol. arrow_*: the arrow's direction(s) as seen by the driver; a diagonal arrow counts as the direction it turns to.
  • other: not a signal for cars (pedestrian, bicycle, bus or tram bar, lane-control X), or not a traffic light at all.
  • unclear: too small, blurred, occluded or overexposed to decide. Prefer it to guessing.
  • Labels autosave in this browser. Export often, and send the JSON when done.
