VZC-TLD review: Exp 7 and moves model

100 images chosen by outcome, 25 per group, so this shows disagreements and is not a sample of the error rate. Each image has three views. VZC label: thick boxes are the relevant lights in their color, thin grey boxes are not relevant, grey means unknown color. Exp 7: boxes are lights assigned to the junction, labelled with pictogram, color and score. Moves model: boxes are relevant lights, labelled with their moves, color and score. Thin grey boxes are lights the model calls not relevant. The decision comes from the highest-scoring light a model calls relevant: red or yellow is stop, green is go, nothing is none.
Keys on the highlighted card: 1 label, 2 Exp 7, 3 moves model; c correct, w wrong, u unsure for the model in view; l VZC label looks wrong; j/k next / previous; n note. Answers stay in this browser; Export JSON saves them.
